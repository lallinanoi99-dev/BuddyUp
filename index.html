Good to know

Do I need a wallet to browse?

No. Browse every post and share links without connecting. You only need a wallet (MetaMask) to post, join, leave or close.

Is my contact public?

Only the handle you choose to add. It's optional, and nothing else about you is stored.

Can I change the number of spots?

No. Spots are fixed once you post. It keeps things fair and simple for everyone who joins.

What's stored on-chain?

The post (title, description, category, area, spots, date, optional handle), who hosted it, and which wallets joined. No payments, no tokens, no private details.
